Learning objectives:
• Identify common warehouse hazards.
• Recognise unsafe pedestrian routes and forklift/pedestrian separation risks.
• Recognise blocked emergency equipment and exits.
• Select appropriate corrective actions.
3D Training objective: identify all 8 hazards across Main Storage, Loading Area, Equipment Area and Emergency Area. The center crosshair uses camera raycasting; not every object is a hazard, so observe before selecting.
How to use: 360° mode — drag to rotate, inspect six hotspots and mark each investigated. 3D mode — WASD move, mouse look, click inspect, ESC pause. Quiz — select one answer per question and submit.
PRESENTATION / DEMO INSTRUCTIONS
1. Start 3D Training and demonstrate WASD + mouse camera movement. 2. Use the TRUE 360° VIEW to show the equirectangular panorama and rotate 360°. 3. Point at the visible 360° hotspots and open their hazard information. 4. Identify hazards in the 3D scene; finish the 3-question quiz. 5. Show the results/review screen and explain the hazard status table.
A mouse and keyboard are recommended. The prototype includes a self-contained local fallback; external CDN libraries are used for Three.js/A-Frame when available.
